The sound card the radio is connected to. Aim for peaks around −12 dBFS.

Transmission detection

−42 dB

The level that counts as speech. Lower it if weak stations are missed, raise it if noise triggers recordings. The threshold is shown as a line in the level meter.

0.7 s

Shorter transmissions are discarded. Counters hallucinations from clicks and squelch tails.

700 ms

Silence before the segment is closed. Longer keeps pauses within one transmission together.

300 ms

Audio kept from before the threshold was crossed, so the first syllable is not cut off.

Set the language explicitly when you know it — auto misses on short transmissions.

Welcome to Commscribe

Connect the radio's speaker output to the sound card, and pick it here. Everything that comes in is split into transmissions and written out as text.

Turn the radio on — the bar should move when someone transmits.

This installation has no audio input — it runs on a server. Upload audio files with Upload or by dropping them into the window, and they are transcribed here, on the server, without anything being sent out.

To log a radio, run the desktop app where the sound card is. It exists for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Choose a speech model

The model runs on your machine. Nothing leaves the building. It is downloaded once.
